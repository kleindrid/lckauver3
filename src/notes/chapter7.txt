body: OFFICIAL STATEMENT SUMMARY — RYU MINSEOK (AUDIO RECEIVED FROM INSPECTOR KANG)
- Sunday Night Fight: When Minseok & Jihoon returned from the market and Kyu got home from work, Kwanghee and Jaehyuk were in the living room. A massive fight broke out (Kyu vs. Kwanghee, and Jaehyuk vs. Kwanghee—neither seemed to know about the relationship).
- Everyone Left the House: Kyu kicked Kwanghee out, Jihoon followed Jaehyuk outside, and Kyu told Minseok to pack Kwanghee's bags and never slept at home that night.
- Abduction (~22:00): Home alone when the doorbell rang; the Rabbit killer attacked Minseok, knocked him unconscious, and tied his wrists in an abandoned, moldy house on the outskirts of town.
- Lure & Confrontation: Kwanghee arrived calling for Minseok (likely lured by the killer). The masked figure held Kwanghee at gunpoint with a raspy, possibly voice-changed voice, mentioning burn scars under the suit caused by Kwanghee's family and insulting Kwanghee's father ("unfaithful, selfish, irresponsible").
- Final Struggle: Minseok slipped one wrist free, tackled the killer, threw the steel revolver across the room, and Kwanghee grabbed it—only to yell for Minseok to run instead of shooting right away.
- Outcome: As Minseok fled, he heard a heavy fall and six rapid gunshots; looking back, only the Rabbit's silhouette remained standing. Outside, he heard the killer walking unhurriedly off the porch—confirming Kwanghee was almost certainly killed. Minseok wandered lost in the woods until dawn before reaching a diner and coming straight to the precinct.
