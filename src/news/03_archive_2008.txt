headline: SPECIAL REPORT (2008): GYEONG-GWAN MOURNS AS HOME INVASION SPREE ENDS WITHOUT ARREST; ONE INFANT STILL MISSING
byline: By Han J. · Gyeong-gwan Herald Archive (November 14, 2008)

---
heading:
text:
GYEONG-GWAN — Two weeks after the final siren faded along Seongnam-ro, Gyeong-gwan remains wrapped in an uneasy silence. What began as a series of unexplained break-ins escalated into the darkest autumn in the city’s history, leaving multiple households slaughtered behind locked doors.

Police investigators confirmed yesterday that the perpetrator—known in witness statements only for a ragged white rabbit costume—has ceased all known activity following the final home invasion on October 29. Despite sweeping roadblocks and door-to-door searches across every district, no suspect was ever apprehended.

---
heading: THE SURVIVORS AND THE VANISHED CHILD
text:
Among the devastating losses, two children survived the worst nights of the rampage. One young boy was pulled from a back closet after witnessing his family’s murder, while older sibling Han Wangho lost his entire household on the night the attacker broke into their residence.

In the most haunting detail of the case file, police confirmed that Wangho’s infant brother vanished from his crib during the chaos of that night. Search dogs lost the scent near the old rail overpass, and no ransom demand was ever received.

---
heading: EVIDENCE SEALED AS COLD CASE
text:
Before disappearing, the assailant discarded the bloodstained rabbit suit inside an abandoned utility shed near the riverfront. Forensics lifted no usable fingerprints or DNA from the weathered fabric.

Chief Inspector Park’s predecessor officially transferred the costume and scene files to the central precinct evidence vault this week. While the city slowly tries to reopen its schools and markets, grieving families say Gyeong-gwan will never truly heal until the killer is found and the missing baby is brought home.
