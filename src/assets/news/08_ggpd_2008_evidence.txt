heading: CANINE TRACKING & WITNESS LOG (OCT 29 – NOV 1, 2008)
text:
Field log 08-412 confirms tracking units followed trace infant scent north from the Han residence for 420 meters before losing the trail beside the Seongnam-ro rail overpass stairs—pointing directly to vehicle or bus transfer.

Transit terminal attendant Kang testified seeing a panicked woman wearing a dark coat carrying a bundled toddler toward the late provincial bus platform at 05:40 AM, roughly 36 hours after the break-in.

---
heading: UNCLOSED CROSS-REFERENCE FLAG
text:
A follow-up memo recommended cross-checking delayed late-2008 home-birth filings across surrounding districts for male infants registered without hospital delivery records, noting a former Old District housekeeper resigned without forwarding address that same week.
