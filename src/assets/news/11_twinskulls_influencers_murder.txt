headline: TRUE-CRIME INFLUENCERS TWINSKULLS FOUND ASSASSINATED IN CAMPUS WOODS
byline: By Kiin · Senior Investigative Desk (Wednesday, Oct 28)

---
heading: 
text:
SEONGNAM-RO, GYEONG-GWAN — Hours after their controversial live broadcast discussing the Gyeong-gwan municipal and precinct murders, the two hosts behind viral true-crime channel TwinSkulls (@TwinSkullsLive) were found dead early this morning inside a camping tent pitched in the dense pine woods directly behind Gyeong-gwan University campus.

Precinct forensic investigators confirmed both victims suffered identical, precise stab wounds delivered with professional execution. A portable streaming rig and ring light were found smashed near the tent flap.

---
heading: LIVE BROADCAST SCREEN GRABS SCRUBBED
text:
According to digital forensics, a live video feed showing the masked assailant known as the 'Bunny Killer' entering the tent, executing the broadcast hosts, and ending the stream remained accessible on public mirror sites until 07:30 AM before federal cyber units forced a total takedown across all platforms.

Residents and university students have expressed widespread panic as the killer demonstrates an escalating boldness, shifting from silent midnight stalking to public broadcast assassinations. Police have urged all students to remain inside residential dorms under strict curfew.
