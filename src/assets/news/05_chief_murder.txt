headline: PRECINCT IN SHOCK: POLICE LIEUTENANT AND WIFE FOUND MURDERED AT HOME BEFORE DAWN; ALL ELECTRONIC EVIDENCE DESTROYED
byline: By Kim K. & Kiin · Gyeong-gwan Crime Desk (Sunday, Oct 25)

---
heading:
text:
GYEONG-GWAN — Tragedy struck directly at the heart of law enforcement before sunrise Sunday morning as the senior Chief Lieutenant leading Gyeong-gwan’s serial homicide task force and his wife were discovered murdered inside their private residence.

Responding officers arrived at the quiet residential lane just before 06:30 AM after neighbors reported shattered study windows and an unlatched side entrance. Both victims succumbed to fatal injuries hours earlier while the neighborhood slept.

---
heading: PARTY VIDEO FOOTAGE AND DEVICES SYSTEMATICALLY SMASHED
text:
According to precinct insiders, the attacker did not steal valuables or money from the household. Instead, every single electronic device inside the lieutenant’s home—personal phones, laptops, external hard drives, and the party video recordings recently submitted as key evidence in Lee Minhyung’s murder—was violently smashed and rendered unreadable.

Sources confirm the lieutenant had taken raw footage from Dohyeon’s residence and the victim’s confiscated mobile phone home late Saturday evening to inspect the video frame by frame.

---
heading: INVESTIGATION CRIPPLED AS FEAR GRIPS STUDENTS
text:
With digital forensics teams unable to recover data from the shattered storage units inside the study, investigators now face the chilling reality that the killer knew exact precinct movements—and struck solely to erase what was recorded at Friday night’s party.

Uniformed patrols have surrounded Gyeong-gwan University dormitories, while panicked students—particularly witnesses questioned yesterday—are packing bags to leave the city altogether.
