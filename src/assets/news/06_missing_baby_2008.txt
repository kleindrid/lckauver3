---
heading: CASE SUMMARY: OCTOBER 29, 2008
text:
Victim Name: Han Infant (Minor Male, abducted at approximately 20 months old during the Gyeong-gwan home invasion). Both parents were fatally assaulted inside the family residence; older sibling Han Wangho survived undetected in an upstairs closet.

Despite nationwide Missing Children South Korea broadcasts in late 2008, no ransom contact was ever made and no remains were ever recovered from the scene or nearby riverfront.

---
heading: OVERLOOKED WITNESS LEAD & REGISTRY ANOMALY
text:
Two days after the Gyeong-gwan home invasion, a nighttime bus terminal attendant reported seeing an agitated woman clutching a crying toddler wrapped in a dark wool blanket near the old rail overpass—roughly 400 meters from where police dogs lost the infant’s scent.

A follow-up civil registry audit six years later flagged a delayed out-of-hospital birth registration filed in a neighboring district during November 2008 under a newly relocated single guardian. Gyeong-gwan Police closed the inquiry without DNA verification after the original lead detective retired.
