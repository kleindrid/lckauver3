---
heading: ENHANCED ENTRY CONTROLS ACROSS BUILDINGS A, B & C
text:
Effective immediately, keycard locks remain engaged 24 hours a day across Dorm 304 and all Campus Flats residential buildings—North Wing (Bldg A), East Courtyard (Bldg B), and Riverside Annex (Bldg C).

Students walking between Humanities Hall, Arts Studio, and Campus Flats after 19:00 are urged to travel in groups or request campus security escorts from the Main Quad kiosk.
